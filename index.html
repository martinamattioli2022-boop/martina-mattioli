// research

Where machine learning meets epistemology

Modern AI produces predictions faster than we can justify them. My work asks what counts as knowledge, explanation and evidence when the knower is a model.

01

Philosophy of explanation in XAI

Drawing on the history of scientific explanation to examine the assumptions behind explainable AI methods and what they can really deliver.

02

XAI in healthcare

What makes an explanation adequate in medicine: causality, trust between clinicians and systems, and the needs of different stakeholders.

03

Foundations of machine learning

Induction, causality and the epistemic status of learning, read through the lens of the philosophy of science.

04

Similarity, relations and categorization

The epistemic role of similarity and relational structure in how models represent and classify the world.

05

Ethics of emotion recognition

A critical look at facial emotion recognition, its technical reliability and its use in ethically sensitive contexts.
